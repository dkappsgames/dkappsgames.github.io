MYCV Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

MYCV ("the app") lets you create, edit and preview resumes and export them as PDF. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and the choices you have. "We" means the publisher of MYCV; contact details are at the end.

Information we collect

DataWhy
Account data: name and email address, and a password if you sign up with email. If you use Google or Apple sign-in, we receive the name, email and account identifier those providers share with us.To create and secure your account and to pre-fill new resumes.
Resume content you enter: names, headlines, contact details, work history, education, skills, dates, free text and any other section content.To store and display your resumes and generate your PDF.
Profile photo you choose from your photo library or take with the camera.To place it on your resume. The app asks permission before accessing the camera or photos.
Usage analytics: sign-in and sign-up events (and which method), screens viewed, device and app information, and an app-instance identifier (Firebase Analytics).To understand how the app is used and improve it.
Crash and error reports: stack traces, device model, OS version, app version and an installation identifier (Firebase Crashlytics).To find and fix bugs.

We do not show ads, do not sell your personal information, and do not use your resume content for advertising or to train AI models.

How your information is stored and shared

We use service providers who process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase – authentication, database and file storage for your account, resumes and photo.
  • Google Firebase (Analytics and Crashlytics) – analytics and crash reporting.
  • Google Sign-In / Sign in with Apple – only if you choose to sign in with them.

PDFs are generated on your device. If you share or print a PDF, it goes wherever you send it. Resume content is private to your account; we do not publish it. We may disclose information if required by law.

Retention and deletion

We keep your account and resume data until you delete it or ask us to delete your account. To delete your account and all associated data, email us at the address below from the email on your account; we will complete the deletion within 30 days. Analytics and crash data are retained by Firebase according to its standard retention periods.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under GDPR, or California under CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Contact us to exercise these rights. You can also edit or delete most of your data directly in the app.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access to stored data is restricted to your account. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

MYCV is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU), and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version; material changes will be announced in the app or by email.
