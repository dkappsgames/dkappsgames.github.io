Delete your MYCV account

MYCV is published by developer Divya Kothari. This page explains how to request deletion of your MYCV account and the data associated with it.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to dkappsgames@gmail.com from the email address you use to sign in to MYCV (including the address of your Google or Apple account if you signed in with one).
  2. Use the subject "MYCV account deletion request". You don't need to give a reason.
  3. We may reply to confirm the request comes from the account owner.
  4. We delete your account and data within 30 days and email you when it's done.

Email a deletion request

If you only want to remove some content, you can edit or delete individual resumes and your photo directly in the app without deleting your account.

What gets deleted

DataWhat happens
Account data: name, email, password, and sign-in identifiers from Google or ApplePermanently deleted within 30 days
All resumes and their content (contact details, work history, education, skills, free text)Permanently deleted within 30 days
Profile photos you uploadedPermanently deleted within 30 days

What may be kept

DataHow long
Usage analytics (Firebase Analytics), linked to an app-instance identifier rather than your accountUp to 14 months, under Firebase's standard retention, then deleted automatically
Crash and error reports (Firebase Crashlytics)Up to 90 days, then deleted automatically
Our email correspondence about your requestKept only as long as needed to show the request was handled

PDFs you exported are stored on your device or wherever you shared them, so we can't delete those copies. Uninstalling the app removes data stored on your device.
